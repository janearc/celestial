# celestial

the physics of the sky, in go, in one place.

celestial gathers what was spread across slayer, libtheme-css and
libholemath, so that each constant, unit and law is written once:

- units and constants, cited, in SI, with conversions to and from the
  natural units some of the physics prefers
- time scales, the calendar, sidereal time, frames and places
- spectra and the atmosphere
- stars, gravitation, and the kerr hole from libholemath
- FITS and HDF5, written here
- LIGO and Virgo: events, the remnant hole, strain

data files live in celestial-data, not here.
